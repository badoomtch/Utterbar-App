# Utterbar 0.23.0

A stability update that fixes a crash triggered by copied images and makes the first dictation after launch clearer and more responsive.

## Fixed

- **Copied-image clipboard crash.** Copying an image, then dictating, could crash Utterbar while it restored your original clipboard. This is fixed. Standard copied screenshots and images are preserved safely after dictation.
- **Recording looked stuck after you stopped.** If you dictated immediately after launch while your model was still preparing, the floating bar could keep showing Recording even though the microphone had already stopped. It now changes to Processing as soon as capture finishes.
- **First dictation gets priority at launch.** Background model preparation waits briefly after launch and steps aside if you start dictating, so it cannot hold up your first recording.
- **Unavailable recording shortcut feedback.** If another app has already claimed your recording shortcut, Utterbar now tells you rather than leaving the shortcut apparently unresponsive.

## What has not changed

- Speech recognition still uses your selected model and acceleration setting. Parakeet has not been forced onto CPU.
- Your dictation language, paste settings, clipboard-restoration delay, end-of-speech delay, audio capture, models, licence and update preferences are unchanged.
- No new network calls, analytics or telemetry have been added.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Please include the log file, your Utterbar version, what you had copied before dictating, and the app you were dictating into when you report an issue.
