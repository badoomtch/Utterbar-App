# Utterbar 0.8.8 (beta)

Two Whisper fixes from real-world use.

## Whisper

- **Whisper now runs on CPU regardless of your GPU preference.** Sherpa-onnx's DirectML provider has incomplete op coverage for Whisper's encoder/decoder graphs — `base` mostly survives, `small` reliably crashes the native layer mid-transcribe (process dies, no usable error). Forcing CPU is slower than ideal but always correct. Parakeet and Zipformer keep your GPU preference; only Whisper is pinned.
- **`[Music]` and friends are filtered out.** Whisper was trained on captioned video where silent/ambient audio gets annotated as `[Music]`, `[Applause]`, `[Background noise]`, `(laughter)` etc. On quiet input it hallucinates these tags as the "transcript". They now get caught and dropped — you'll see no text inserted instead of `[Music]` pasted into your app. Stock confabulated phrases ("Thanks for watching", "you", isolated punctuation) are also filtered.

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log and any crash dumps. Send the newest files to support and we'll take a look.
