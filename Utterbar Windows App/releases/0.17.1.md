# Utterbar 0.17.1 (beta)

A small visual tune on top of v0.17.0. Stable channel still serves v0.17.0.

## What this changes

The floating bar's seven waveform bars are now more responsive to normal speech. In v0.17.0 the bars sat near the bottom of their range for a typical user voice. In this beta:

- **Quiet and normal speech show more visible movement.** The bars swing further on regular conversational volume, so you can see at a glance that the mic is picking you up.
- **Soft speech shows some motion** rather than barely flickering off the floor.
- **Silence still sits low.** Dead air keeps the bars at the bottom row, so the bar never falsely says "I'm hearing you" when it isn't.
- **Reduced motion behaviour is unchanged.** If you have Windows reduce-motion enabled (or the in-app accessibility setting), the bars still freeze at a static row exactly as in v0.17.0.

## What this does not change

Your transcripts and your privacy posture are untouched:

- **Speech recognition accuracy is unchanged.** Only the visual responsiveness of the bars in the floating window is different. The audio Utterbar listens to and the text it produces are exactly the same as v0.17.0.
- **No changes to audio capture or microphone routing.** Utterbar still reads your voice the same way.
- **No changes to the speech engine or the speech models.** Same on-device speech recognition as v0.17.0.
- **No changes to GPU acceleration or the provider policy.**
- **No telemetry or analytics added.** Utterbar still sends nothing about your usage to us.
- **True per-app microphone mute is still NOT in this release.** Output-audio mute only, same as v0.17.0.

## Beta channel

Beta channel only. Stable channel still serves v0.17.0. No release announcement email, no `latest` tag.
