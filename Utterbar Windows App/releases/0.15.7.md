# Utterbar 0.15.7 (beta)

A small UX fix on top of v0.15.6: regular dictation now stops itself just before the 5-minute cap instead of refusing the recording at the end.

## What this fixes

Regular (single-shot) dictation has a hard 5-minute cap on the offline recogniser. Up to v0.15.6, if you held the hotkey past the cap, the app refused the recording at stop time and your captured audio was lost.

v0.15.7 stops the recording 5 seconds before the cap and transcribes everything captured up to that point. The floating bar shows "Auto-stopped at 5 min" so you can see why the recording ended without your hotkey press.

## What changes

- Single-shot dictation auto-stops at 4:55. The captured audio is transcribed and inserted normally.
- The floating bar surfaces the auto-stop with a clear non-error label instead of falling through to the generic "Inserted" pill.
- Meeting Mode is unaffected. Meetings still cap at roughly an hour, with chunked transcription end-to-end.

## What this does not change

- The underlying 5-minute cap on the offline recogniser. The streaming-recogniser rebuild that lifts the cap is still on the roadmap.
- Pricing, licence system, updater, history persistence, file transcription, the floating bar visuals.
- Local-only. No audio leaves your machine.
- Your transcripts. Same audio in, same text out.

## Beta only

Beta-channel release. No `--announce`, no `latest` tag. Stable channel still serves v0.15.6.
