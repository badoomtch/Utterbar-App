# Utterbar 0.6.3

Polish pass on top of 0.6.2 — same stability story, a real history redesign, and smoother UI throughout.

## New

- **History redesigned.** One full-width column of entries instead of a cramped two-column split that squeezed the list to 200 px on normal window sizes. Click any entry to open the detail in a modal — raw transcript, processed output, reprocess-as-mode, and context inspection all live there. Works at any window size.

## Fixed

- **Floating bar opens and closes in one smooth motion.** The pill width, icon opacity, and icon position now finish within a single 280 ms window instead of running on three separate springs that finished at different times. No more staggered stages on hover-in, hover-out, record start, or record stop.
- **Border lights up cyan while you drag the bar.** The whole pill perimeter now glows the accent colour for the duration of the drag, not just an inner ring. Visible the instant you click-and-hold.
- **Third scrollbar on small window sizes, gone.** The main window now has at most two scrollbars (one per column) at any size. Previously a third bar appeared on narrow widths, scrolling the left menu from the wrong place.
- **Free-tier "Full history is Pro" message fits on screen.** Now a full-width card above the list instead of a cramped half-width nag that wrapped one word per line.
