# Utterbar 0.13.0 (beta)

Meeting Mode. Beta channel only — soaking before promotion to stable.

## What it does

Pick a mode flagged as a Meeting (the built-in **Meeting** mode is set up that way out of the box) and Utterbar shifts how it records and transcribes:

- **Mic + system audio together, automatically.** Recording always uses both — your microphone and whatever is playing through your speakers, summed into a single track. No need to switch the global capture source. The point of Meeting Mode is "press record on a Zoom or Teams call and get both sides without hunting through Audio settings."
- **Long recordings get chunked.** The captured audio is split into 30-second windows on stop and transcribed window by window through your offline model. A 45-minute meeting produces 90 chunks, stitched back together at the end. Memory stays flat — none of the 30-min decoder crashes that put 0.12.1 out — and the 15-minute offline cap doesn't apply because no single transcribe call sees more than 30 seconds.
- **Verbatim transcript, no LLM polish.** Post-processing is skipped: no filler-word stripping, no template wrapping, no auto-punctuation rewrite. Meetings are reference material. You want the literal record of what was said, not a polished prose version. English variant (BrE / AmE) still applies because it's a spelling fix, not a rewrite.

## How to use it

Two paths.

**Quick path:** Settings → Modes → tap **Meeting**. That's it. Hotkey + speak + stop, same as any dictation, except now it'll handle a long recording without choking.

**Per-mode path:** Settings → Modes → open any mode → flip "Treat this mode as a meeting capture" on, save. The mode will now use Meeting Mode behaviour. Useful if you want the meeting flag on a mode that has different vocabulary or English variant settings from the built-in.

If you ever set Settings → Audio → Capture source to System or Mixed manually, you'll see a callout pointing at Meeting Mode — which is a smoother way to get the same thing without leaving system audio capture on for everyday dictation.

## Stitching caveat

Chunk boundaries fall every 30 seconds of wall clock, with a 2-second overlap. The overlap exists so the recogniser doesn't cleave a word at the boundary; the stitch step then drops the first ~6.7% of words from each non-first chunk to avoid duplicates. This is imperfect — words don't distribute evenly across an audio chunk — so you may see a missing word at one boundary or a duplicate at another. For a 30-minute meeting that's at most a handful of single-word glitches across thousands of words. We have a longest-common-substring stitch on the roadmap as a future polish.

## Why beta

Two reasons:

- The 30-second chunk size and 2-second overlap are both "first reasonable guesses." We need real meeting recordings to confirm that's the right pair before promoting to stable. If the stitch quality on real conversations is worse than expected, the boundary tuning happens against soaked beta data, not stable users.
- Meeting Mode forces system-audio capture every time it runs. That's the right default but it's a behaviour change people might not expect on first try. The beta soak gives the docs + the cross-suggestion callout time to land before the stable channel sees it.

When the meeting transcription quality holds up over a couple of weeks of beta use, this gets republished to the stable channel.

## Carry-overs

- The 15-minute cap on the offline transcribe path stays in place for non-meeting modes. Meeting Mode bypasses it; everything else still gets the safety net against the long-input decoder crash.
- The streaming Zipformer experiment from 0.12.2 has been removed — it shipped as a beta, didn't hold up under real use (live transcripts in the floating bar were noise, the model output was uppercase tokens), and Meeting Mode's chunked-offline path covers the same use case with the user's preferred model and gold-standard accuracy. The 0.12.2 installer remains on the dl.utterbar.com archive as a historical artefact; nothing on the stable channel ever saw it.

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log and any crash dumps. Send the newest files to `hello@ddraig.dev` and we'll take a look. The thing to watch for in particular: memory growth past ~500 MB during a long meeting (should stay flat), or the chunked transcribe taking dramatically longer than the recording itself (each chunk should run ~real-time on Parakeet / offline Zipformer; Whisper is slower).
