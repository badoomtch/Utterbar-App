# Utterbar 0.6.2

The stability release. Fixes the crashes from 0.6.1 plus a handful of smaller annoyances.

## Fixed

- **Crashes on repeated dictation.** A single hotkey press was firing the start-recording command multiple times under the hood, which raced three microphone streams and made the app corrupt its own memory. Reliable within a few minutes of use on 0.6.1. This is gone now — one press, one recording.
- **The start-stop cue played up to six times per press.** Same underlying cause. Fixed alongside the crash.
- **Transcription making up filler words.** If you tapped the hotkey by accident or stopped too early, the transcriber would sometimes insert a phantom "yeah" / "so" / "mm" / "uh" from the room tone. Those are now caught and dropped before anything reaches your clipboard.
- **Floating bar icons disappeared the moment you moved the mouse off.** They now ease out smoothly instead of popping.
- **Floating bar didn't light up while you were dragging it.** The accent ring now comes on the instant you click and hold.
- **Main window had three scrollbars** when the window was small. Now just one on each side where you'd expect.
- **In-app Pro price showed `$29`** on first launch before the real price arrived from the server. It now shows the correct £39.99 from the start.
