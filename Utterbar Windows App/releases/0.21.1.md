# Utterbar 0.21.1 (beta)

A reliability beta on top of v0.21.0. Stable users remain on v0.21.0.

## Read this first

This beta focuses on three problems reported during ordinary dictation: a transcript occasionally not appearing in the target app, the clipboard occasionally not returning to what you had copied before dictating, and final words sometimes being cut short.

## More reliable text insertion

Utterbar now checks Windows input delivery more strictly. If Windows reports that text could not be inserted, the floating bar tells you instead of claiming success. Your transcript is still saved in History, where you can copy it straight away.

The automatic Enter press used by auto-send only runs after successful text insertion. That stops a failed paste from also sending an unexpected message in the target app.

## Better clipboard restoration

When Utterbar temporarily uses the clipboard to paste your transcript, it now gives slower apps more time to receive the text before restoring what you had copied previously. It also leaves the clipboard alone if you or another app copied something new in the meantime.

## Less risk of losing final words

Utterbar keeps audio capture open for a short grace period after you stop dictating. The microphone path can extend that period briefly while speech is still arriving, with a firm limit so stopping remains responsive. Meeting Mode applies the same final-frame protection to system audio, and stops its microphone and system-audio capture together.

## What this release does not change

- **No speech-recognition model change.** Whisper, Parakeet, Zipformer, model files, and model checksums are unchanged.
- **No change to Meeting Mode transcription behaviour** beyond retaining the final captured audio more reliably.
- **No change to the App language picker or interface translations.**
- **No change to graphics acceleration, microphone selection, or noise handling.**
- **No change to the licence system, Stripe, price, or activation flow.**
- **No new outbound network calls, analytics, telemetry, or local LLM rewrite.**
- **Stable users remain on v0.21.0** unless they select the beta channel in Settings, Updates.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Please email hello@ddraig.dev with the log file, the version from Settings, About, the target app you dictated into, and what you expected to happen.

For a missing final word, include the full sentence you spoke, what appeared, and whether the same result happens again. For a missing paste or clipboard problem, include whether automatic paste and clipboard restoration were enabled.

## Beta channel

Beta channel only. Stable channel still serves v0.21.0. No release announcement email and no `latest` tag.
