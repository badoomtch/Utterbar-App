# Utterbar 0.15.6

If you've been on stable since v0.12.0, this release brings everything from the beta line in one go. GPU acceleration is the main new piece. Meeting Mode has its own page, a model picker, and works with system audio. The microphone you pick in settings is now used for every recording. Notes below cover the lot.

## What's new since v0.12.0

- DirectML execution provider. Whisper Medium, Whisper Large, and Parakeet can run on the GPU on hardware that has one. New in v0.15.6.
- Whisper Large v3 and Whisper Medium added to the model catalogue. Pro-tier; pick what suits your hardware.
- Meeting Mode, with its own page, timestamped transcripts, and a model picker.
- Meeting Mode captures the microphone and system audio together, so a Zoom or Teams call records both sides without changing your audio settings.
- The microphone you pick in Settings > Audio is the one that records. If you'd selected a USB interface and recordings still came from the laptop's built-in mic, that's fixed.
- Updates page is its own sidebar entry, with the release notes for the version you're running.
- Floating bar mode switcher. Favourite a mode to put it on the bar.

## Meeting Mode

Pick a mode flagged as a Meeting (the built-in Meeting mode is set up that way out of the box, or flip the meeting flag on any custom mode) and Utterbar records the microphone and the system audio together into one summed track. Press record on a Zoom or Teams call and you get both sides of the conversation.

Long recordings are chunked. The captured audio is split into 29-second windows on stop and transcribed window by window. Memory stays flat across the whole recording.

### Meetings page

A dedicated Meetings entry in the sidebar, separate from /history. Cards list each meeting with date, duration, and a one-line preview. Click into a meeting to see the full timestamped transcript:

```
[00:00] Welcome to the call.
[00:14] Sounds good.
[00:42] Let's start with the agenda.
```

From the detail view: Copy to clipboard, export as `.txt` or `.md` (with a small YAML front-matter block: date, duration, mode), or Delete the meeting. The Meetings page refreshes itself when a recording finishes. Stay on the page, finish a meeting, the new card appears.

### Meeting Mode model picker

In Settings > Models > Meeting mode model, pick one of three options:

- Fast, Whisper Small (~640 MB). Recommended; comfortably faster than realtime on most laptops. Less accurate than Medium on proper nouns and jargon, but transcribes everyday English cleanly.
- Balanced, Whisper Medium (~1.9 GB). Slower, better on names and uncommon words.
- Best accuracy, Whisper Large v3 (~1 GB tarball). Most accurate, runs at roughly real-time on CPU. The right pick if you want maximum quality and don't mind the wait.

Meeting Mode only accepts these three Whisper models. Parakeet, Zipformer, and Whisper Base are rejected with a clear error. The whole point of Meeting Mode is Whisper for accuracy on long recordings, and the allow-list enforces that.

If you upgrade and your first meeting refuses to start, you don't have a meeting model installed yet. One click in Settings > Models to pick and install one, then meetings work normally.

### Success pill on the bar

When a meeting wraps, the floating bar shows what just happened:

> Saved · 5:01 · Whisper Small · 11 chunks · 799 chars

Until the Meetings page is open, this is your "did the meeting record" signal.

### Meeting cap of about 60 minutes

A single Meeting Mode recording is capped at roughly an hour (60 min 47 s, to be exact: 135 chunks of 29 s plus a small shoulder). Standard 30, 45, and 60 minute meetings fit comfortably. Anything longer should be split into separate recordings.

### Speaker labels are disabled

Meeting Mode transcripts come back with timestamps, no speaker labels. Earlier attempts at voice-timbre clustering produced wrong-shaped output, and the rework is planned for a future release. The infrastructure is in tree but isn't shipping yet. An honest "we don't know who's speaking" instead of a confident wrong guess.

## DirectML execution provider

Settings > Advanced has had a GPU acceleration card since v0.6 with Auto, DirectML, CUDA, and CPU radio buttons. Until v0.15.5 the card claimed running on the GPU was 3 to 10 times faster, but the shipping ONNX Runtime in every stable release back to v0.7.0 was CPU-only. The DirectML radio did nothing. v0.15.5 made the gap visible with a "Selected, but currently inactive" notice. v0.15.6 makes DirectML available.

### What runs on the GPU on Auto

When Settings > Advanced > GPU acceleration is on Auto and your machine has a DirectX 12 graphics card:

- Whisper Medium runs on DirectML.
- Whisper Large v3 runs on DirectML.
- Parakeet TDT v3 int8 runs on DirectML.
- Whisper Small stays on CPU. Real-meeting testing showed Small is faster on the CPU than on the GPU at every audio length we measured. The per-session GPU init cost dominates Small's short compute window. If you specifically want Small on the GPU, set Settings > Advanced > GPU acceleration to DirectML, not Auto. Explicit DirectML overrides Auto's per-model rules.

If your machine has no DX12 GPU, Auto runs everything on CPU.

### Performance on Jay's machine

| Engine | CPU RTF | DML RTF | Speedup |
|---|---|---|---|
| Whisper Small | 0.143 | 0.183 | 0.78x (DML slower) |
| Whisper Medium | 0.362 | 0.200 | 1.81x |
| Whisper Large v3 | 0.734 | 0.267 | 2.75x |
| Parakeet TDT v3 int8 | 0.086 | 0.060 | 1.43x |

Speedup grows with model size. Your hardware will land somewhere different.

### First-run cost on the GPU path

The first time DirectML uses a model in a session, the GPU has to compile the model graph for your hardware. This is a one-off cost paid lazily on the first dictation or meeting after launch:

- Parakeet (dictation): roughly 20 to 25 seconds before the first transcript appears.
- Whisper Medium or Large: roughly 30 to 60 seconds on top of the transcribe itself for the first meeting; can stretch longer on the first chunk.

After that the recogniser is cached, and subsequent dictations or meetings start straight away. Quitting the app loses the cache, and the next launch pays the cold cost again on first use.

If a meeting feels stuck at "Warming…" or "Processing…" for the first minute, that's this. Not a hang. Let it finish.

### Hardware requirements

- A Direct3D 12 capable GPU. NVIDIA GTX 10-series and newer, AMD RX 400/500-series and newer, Intel Arc, Intel integrated (UHD Graphics 600+), AMD integrated (Vega+).
- Windows 10 1903+ or Windows 11.
- If no DX12 GPU is detected, Auto runs on CPU.

### Settings > Advanced

The card now describes what the resolver actually does on this build, including the per-model Auto behaviour. If your machine has no DX12 GPU, DirectML still appears in the picker but the row carries an inactive notice explaining that transcribes are running on CPU. Your preference is preserved either way.

### Provider telemetry

Every meeting and dictation log line in `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` records six fields about the provider decision: `provider_requested`, `provider_used`, `fallback_used`, `fallback_reason`, `dml_available`, and `provider_policy_reason`. Useful for diagnosing performance or "did GPU acceleration kick in for this run?" without the app's UI.

## Audio and microphone

### The selected microphone is used

If you opened Settings > Audio, picked your USB interface, and noticed recordings still came from the laptop array mic, that's fixed. Recording now honours your selection across every entry point: floating bar, hotkey, onboarding, tray menu. If the configured device isn't available (unplugged, renamed in Sound settings), Utterbar falls back to the host default and shows a Windows toast naming both devices, so you're not silently recording from the wrong mic.

### Whisper Large v3 doesn't crash on first use

The launch crash some users hit when Whisper Large was first added is fixed. Whisper Large now runs to completion at any language setting. The Whisper-language pin in Settings > Models > Whisper language still works. Set it to English if Whisper ever invents random foreign-script content on quiet audio.

### Diagnostic audio dump

Off by default. Flip `audio.diagnostic_audio_dump` to `true` in `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\settings.json` and every recording writes the raw and processed PCM to `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\diagnostics\audio\`. Useful only when reproducing a specific audio-quality bug for support. Keeps the most recent six pairs and rotates older files out automatically.

## Mode switcher in the floating bar

The floating bar shows mode chips between the state pill and the action buttons (when not recording). Click a chip to switch into that mode. The active mode is accent-tinted.

A mode appears in the bar when its favourite flag is on. Star a mode in Settings > Modes to add it to the bar; unstar to remove it. New installs default the three built-ins (Voice to Text, Message, Meeting) to favourited.

Switching is intentionally disabled mid-recording. The chips only appear in the bar's idle state.

## Settings > Updates

Update channel, auto-check, and the check-now button are their own sidebar entry, separate from General Settings. The page also shows the release notes for the version you're running, a clear warning if you're on the beta channel, and the release-email subscription toggle.

## Settings > Modes

- Output style (post-processing) is renamed from "none / deterministic" to Plain and Templated, with a live preview of what your format string produces with a sample sentence.
- Icon picker is a grid of 24 lucide icons grouped by use-case instead of a free-text input.
- Auto-activation rule editor has clearer match-type labels (Exe name, Window title, Window class) and a live indicator dot that turns green when the rule fires against your currently focused window. Alt-tab and watch it update in real time.
- Filter dropdowns on the History page no longer clip option labels.

## Settings > Licence

Your Pro key sits behind a Show button instead of being printed in plain text. Auto-rehides after 30 seconds. Copy works whether revealed or hidden. Screen readers always read the real key on focus.

## File transcription

Every completed file-transcription job writes `.srt` and `.vtt` sidecars next to the existing `.utterbar.txt`. So `meeting.mp4` becomes `meeting.utterbar.txt` plus `meeting.utterbar.srt` plus `meeting.utterbar.vtt`. Timing is approximate at the sentence level (the underlying ASR doesn't surface per-word timestamps). Good enough as a rough pass to refine in Subtitle Edit, Premiere, Resolve, or your editor of choice.

## History page

- Filters by Mode, App, and date range (Today, 7 days, 30 days, All). Combine with the search box; a "Reset filters" link appears when anything is set.
- Highlight a misheard word or phrase in the History detail modal and click "Add to vocabulary" to store it as a hint, or "Replace with…" to create a literal replacement applied to every future dictation. Either action triggers a one-click reprocess of the current entry so you can see the fix take effect immediately.
- Meetings live on /meetings now and don't clutter /history.

## For IT teams

Utterbar can be deployed across a managed fleet. Five behaviours are pinnable via the Windows registry under `HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Policies\Utterbar`, overriding the per-user setting on every read:

- `UpdateChannel`: `stable` or `beta`.
- `CheckUpdatesAutomatically`: `0` or `1`.
- `BlockModelDownloads`: refuses any new model download.
- `BlockSystemAudio`: forces capture source back to Microphone.
- `RequireLicence`: refuses to dictate unless a Pro licence is active.

Full deployment doc, including silent install, network egress, and air-gapped activation: https://utterbar.com/docs/it-deployment. Volume pricing on https://utterbar.com/pricing.

If you want a code-signed installer for a fleet rollout, mention it on the business enquiry form. We sign at order time and fold the cert cost into the quote.

## Fixed since v0.12.0

- A pre-publish QA on this release found a hard panic when a Parakeet dictation contained the £ sign. Fixed before publish; v0.15.6 doesn't crash on that input. If you ever hit a similar panic, `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\crash.log` is the file to send to support.
- Path-injection fix on the diagnostics-folder opener buttons.
- Model deletion path safety. The uninstall and retirement-prune flows now reject absolute paths and `..` traversals before removing anything, and confirm the canonicalised target lives inside the models folder.
- Markdown rendering on the website strips raw HTML tags and neutralises `javascript:`, `data:`, and `vbscript:` URLs in release notes and blog posts.
- Release publishing now recomputes the SHA-256 from the CDN object after upload and refuses any release whose pasted hash doesn't match.
- Hotkey-to-listening latency is back to near-instant.
- Offline Zipformer model retired from the catalogue. Parakeet covers the same English fast-path. If you had Zipformer installed, the upgrade prunes it cleanly.

## Known notes

### 5-minute cap on single-shot dictation

Regular dictation refuses inputs longer than 5 minutes with a clear error. Until a streaming-recogniser rebuild ships, this cap protects the offline recogniser from a long-input crash.

If you're recording a long session, use Meeting Mode instead. It chunks internally and handles up to roughly an hour. Single-shot dictation is the hotkey-press-and-speak path; Meeting Mode is the press-record-on-a-call path.

### Speaker labels still off

See Meeting Mode above. Transcripts come back timestamped, no labels. The rework is planned for a future release.

### CUDA still inactive

DirectML covers every DX12 GPU already. CUDA would be marginally faster on NVIDIA but requires a separate ~150 MB runtime, which isn't worth the install size for an NVIDIA-only gain. If the picker shows CUDA selected on a build that doesn't ship the CUDA runtime, the inactive notice explains.

### Installer is unsigned

SmartScreen warnings are expected on first install. Choose "More info" then "Run anyway". An EV signing cert is on the roadmap for B2B fleet rollouts; the current public installer is unsigned.

### Installer size

The DirectML bundle adds ~18.5 MB of `DirectML.dll` plus a slightly larger `onnxruntime.dll` (DML build vs CPU build). Total runtime DLL footprint goes from ~14.9 MB to ~33.8 MB. NSIS LZMA compression brings the on-the-wire installer-size delta to roughly +12 to +15 MB.

## What this does not change

- Pricing. Still £39.99 GBP one-time Pro, 3-device cap.
- Licence system, updater, history persistence, mode definitions, vocabulary, file transcription, the floating bar.
- Local-only. No audio leaves your machine. No cloud, no account for the app, no subscription.
- Your transcripts on CPU. Same audio in, same text out.
- Your transcripts on DirectML are bit-identical to CPU on Whisper Medium and Large v3. Parakeet output may differ slightly because of GPU/CPU decoder-state non-determinism. Both providers produce equivalent quality.

## If you hit a problem

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log. `crash.log` has any panic traces. Every meeting and dictation log line carries the full provider telemetry described under DirectML, useful for "did GPU acceleration run on this attempt?".

If the GPU path crashes, switch to Settings > Advanced > GPU acceleration: CPU and email `hello@ddraig.dev` with the log file from `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` and the dump from `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\crash-*.dmp` if one was produced.
