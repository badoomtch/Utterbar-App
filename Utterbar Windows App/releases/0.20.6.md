# Utterbar 0.20.6 (beta)

A beta release on top of v0.20.5 that adds the Whisper Turbo model and makes Settings tell you which processor your dictation actually uses. Stable users remain on v0.20.0.

## Read this first

This is a beta release. Stable users stay on v0.20.0 unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates. Nothing about how your existing models transcribe has changed.

## What this release adds

**A new model: Whisper Turbo.** Whisper Turbo gets close to Whisper Large v3 accuracy but is much faster, and it works in around 99 languages. You can install it from Settings, Models. It is a good all-round pick if you want Whisper quality without waiting as long as Large v3, and on the Auto graphics setting it runs on your GPU.

**Settings now tells you which processor dictation will use.** Open Settings, Advanced and, for your current model, you will now see whether dictation will run on your CPU or your GPU, and a short reason. The most useful case this clears up: Whisper Small runs on your CPU even when you have a graphics card, because it is actually faster on the CPU for short clips. Settings now says so plainly and points you at the models (Whisper Medium, Whisper Large, Whisper Turbo, or Parakeet) that do use your GPU.

**The CUDA option no longer misleads.** Utterbar uses DirectML for graphics acceleration, which works on any modern graphics card. CUDA has never been included in the app, but the CUDA option was still selectable and quietly ran on your CPU. It is now clearly marked as not available in this build, so picking a graphics option does what it says.

## What this release does not change

- **No change to transcription accuracy or your existing models.** Parakeet, and Whisper base, small, medium, and large v3, all behave exactly as on v0.20.5. Whisper Turbo is a new, separate download; nothing you already have is altered.
- **No change to how long recordings are handled.** The v0.20.5 fix for long single dictations still applies, and Whisper Turbo uses it too.
- **No change to the App language picker, no translation changes, and no new interface languages.** The interface is still English.
- **No change to the auto-update flow, the experimental Teams and Discord microphone mute, the licence system, or Stripe.**
- **No new outbound network calls from the app** beyond downloading a model when you choose to install Whisper Turbo.
- **Stable users remain on v0.20.0** unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. If something looks wrong, email hello@ddraig.dev with the log file, which app you were dictating into, and a short example of what you said and what appeared.

Beta channel only. Stable channel still serves v0.20.0. No release announcement email, no "latest" tag.
