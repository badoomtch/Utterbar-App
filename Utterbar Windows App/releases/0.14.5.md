# Utterbar 0.14.5 (beta)

Floating bar layout fix. Beta channel.

## Bar no longer clips at the edge

In v0.14.4 the new mode quick-switcher pushed the bar's expanded width past what the floating window could hold (520 px window, 600 px min-width pill). The right edge clipped behind the chrome and you couldn't reach the rightmost icons.

The artificial min-width is gone. The bar now sizes itself to whatever's actually inside it, so adding or removing modes from the quick-switcher just makes the bar a little wider or narrower without ever overflowing.

## Mode chips show only favourites

The bar now renders one chip per favourited mode, in your sort order. No cap, no extra rules; the favourite flag is the single switch.

New installs default every built-in (Voice to Text, Message, Meeting) to favourited so the bar isn't empty out of the box. From there you tap the star next to any mode in Settings → Modes to add or remove it from the bar:

- star outlined: not in the bar
- star filled: in the bar

Existing installs that have never explicitly favourited a built-in will get the same default flipped on once on first launch of v0.14.5. If you've already curated favourites in v0.14.4 (favourited at least one built-in), your selections are kept untouched.

A short hint sits above the modes list to make this connection visible without forcing you to discover it: "Tap the star next to a mode to show or hide it in the floating bar's quick switcher."

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log. Send the newest files to `hello@ddraig.dev`.
