# Utterbar 0.17.2 (beta)

A small visual follow-up to v0.17.1. Stable channel still serves v0.17.0.

## What this changes

The floating bar's seven waveform bars now echo the Utterbar logo silhouette while you are speaking, instead of all rising to roughly the same height. v0.17.1 already lifted the bars further on normal speech; this beta reshapes how the seven bars relate to each other:

- **Logo-shape silhouette while speaking.** The middle bars climb higher than the outer bars, so a typical voice draws the same "mountain peak in the middle" shape you see in the Utterbar logo.
- **A touch more sensitive overall.** Normal-volume speech now drives the bars closer to the upper rail than in v0.17.1, while silence still sits low and reduced motion still freezes the row.

## What this does not change

- **Speech recognition accuracy is unchanged.** Only the visual responsiveness and silhouette of the floating-bar waveform is different. The audio Utterbar listens to and the text it produces are exactly the same as v0.17.0 and v0.17.1.
- **No changes to audio capture or microphone routing.**
- **No changes to the speech engine or the speech models.**
- **No changes to GPU acceleration or the provider policy.**
- **No telemetry or analytics added.** Utterbar still sends nothing about your usage to us.
- **True per-app microphone mute is still NOT in this release.** Output-audio mute only, same as v0.17.0 and v0.17.1.

## Beta channel

Beta channel only. Stable channel still serves v0.17.0. No release announcement email, no `latest` tag.
