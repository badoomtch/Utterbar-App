# Utterbar 0.7.0

The first proper update since 0.6.1. Lots of small polish, a big stability fix, and a handful of genuine new behaviours. If you've been on 0.6.1 since it shipped, this is the update to grab.

## Stability

- **Random crashes are gone.** An intermittent crash that could kill the app after a few minutes of normal use has been identified and fixed.
- **Recording no longer stops itself mid-dictation.** If you started a new recording within a second or so of the previous one finishing, the bar would sometimes cut the new one short and require a second press. Fixed.
- **Hotkey no longer silently gets wedged.** If something odd happens mid-dictation, the app now self-heals within a few seconds instead of requiring a restart.

## Floating bar

- **Open and close reads as one smooth motion.** The expand / collapse animation no longer closes in visible stages.
- **Collapsed pill hugs its content** instead of floating in a wide gradient.
- **Recording indicator dot no longer clipped** by the pill's edge.
- **Dragging the empty middle of the expanded pill** moves the window without accidentally triggering a close.
- **Icons fade out smoothly on unhover** instead of popping out instantly.
- **Floating bar remembers its last position.** After every drag the position is saved. On next launch the bar reopens where you left it — unless the saved location is no longer valid (monitor unplugged, resolution change), in which case it falls back to the default. The tray's "Reset position" option still clears it instantly.
- **Recording border stays lit for the whole drag** instead of flashing and vanishing.

## Main window

- **History page redesigned.** Single full-width list of entries; click any to open the detail in a modal. Works at any window size.
- **App version shown directly beneath the "About" item** in the left sidebar, in addition to the existing footer version and the About page.
- **Scrolling to the bottom of long settings pages works properly.** Previously some panels could leave the last option out of reach.
- **Toggle switches look right when they're on.** The knob was sitting slightly off the track edge in the on-state.
- **Sidebar footer stays visible on short windows** — the version number and "local only" strap no longer get cut off.

## Transcription

- **"No audio detected" when you haven't actually said anything.** Tap the hotkey by accident, stay quiet, and you'll see a clear "no audio detected" message instead of the app pretending it inserted nothing.
- **Cold-load status.** The first dictation after launch can take 5-12 seconds for the engine to start. The bar now shows "Initialising model" during that window so it's clear what's happening — no more wondering whether the app has hung.
- **British mode stops turning every "check" into "cheque".** It was confidently wrong in most contexts. Other British spellings (`colour`, `organise`, `favourite`, etc.) still work as before.
- **Fewer phantom filler words.** If you tapped the hotkey by accident or stopped too early, the transcriber sometimes inserted a made-up "um" or "yeah" from the room tone. Those are now caught and dropped.

## Modes

- **Per-mode settings no longer silently override your global settings.** The per-mode ASR model picker and per-mode context toggles (clipboard / selection / active-app) have been removed from the Mode editor — the global Models and Privacy settings are now the single source for those choices. No more flipping a mode toggle and wondering why nothing changed.

## Licence

- **Your licence now persists across app restarts properly.** Previously, licences could occasionally fail to survive a restart, sending the app back to Free even though the key was accepted. Fixed.
- **In-app Pro price shows £39.99** in the pre-connection fallback state.

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log and any crash dumps. Send the newest files to support and we'll take a look.
