# Utterbar 0.20.11 (beta)

A beta release that transcribes meetings live, as they happen, so stopping is quick instead of a long wait. Stable users remain on v0.20.0.

## Read this first

This is a beta release. Stable users stay on v0.20.0 unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates.

## Meetings now transcribe live, as you talk

Until now, Meeting Mode recorded the whole meeting and only started transcribing when you pressed stop, so a long meeting could sit for a few minutes afterwards with the app tied up.

Now the transcript builds up during the meeting. A live panel appears in the main window and fills in speaker by speaker (You, and Others) as people talk, with a timestamp on each line. Because the work happens while you are still in the meeting, stopping is quick: there is only the last moment or two left to catch up on, rather than the whole recording.

A few things worth knowing:

- **Keep the main window open** during the meeting to watch the transcript build. You can close the panel with the X in its corner, and it reappears on your next meeting.
- **It is still on-device.** Nothing about this sends your meeting anywhere. It is the same local transcription, just done as you go instead of all at the end.
- **On a slower computer** the transcript may finish catching up in the few seconds after you stop, rather than being instant. It will not be the long frozen wait it was before, and you can watch its progress the whole time.
- **The finished meeting** is still saved and shown on the Meetings page exactly as before, where you can rename the speakers.

## What this release does not change

- **No change to everyday dictation.** Single-shot dictation and your text settings behave exactly as before. This only affects modes flagged as a Meeting.
- **No change to which models are available**, to the graphics options, or to accuracy. The live transcript uses your chosen meeting model just as the previous release did.
- **No change to the App language picker, no translation changes, and no new interface languages.**
- **No change to the auto-update flow, the experimental Teams and Discord microphone mute, the licence system, or Stripe.**
- **No new outbound network calls from the app** beyond downloading a model when you choose to install one.
- **Stable users remain on v0.20.0** unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Email hello@ddraig.dev with the log file and a short description.
