# Utterbar 0.24.0

This release makes the first recording after launch clearer and keeps your selected speech model ready by default.

## What changed

- **Stopping now responds straight away.** When you release your recording hotkey, the floating bar switches out of Recording immediately. Utterbar still keeps a short audio tail in the background so the final words of your sentence are not cut off.
- **Your model stays ready by default.** New installs now keep the selected speech model loaded until you quit Utterbar. That avoids another model initialisation after a short break. You can still change this in Settings, Models if you prefer to free memory between dictations.
- **Better first recording after launch.** If you dictate soon after opening Utterbar, background preparation no longer starts a second model load while your first recording is being processed. Once the model is ready, later dictations use it directly.

## What has not changed

- Existing model-retention settings are left as they are.
- The End-of-speech delay still protects the end of your sentence. Settings, Audio controls its base amount, and Utterbar may extend it briefly while you are still speaking.
- Speech recognition, dictation language, audio capture, GPU acceleration, paste settings, clipboard restoration, licences and updates work as before.
- No new network calls, analytics or telemetry have been added.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Please include the log file, your Utterbar version, the model you selected, and the steps that led to the problem when you report it.
