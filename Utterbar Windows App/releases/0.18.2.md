# Utterbar 0.18.2 (beta)

A Settings beta on top of v0.18.1. Stable channel still serves v0.18.0.

## Read this first

**Experimental. Do not test this in a real call until you have verified it with a safe dummy call.**

This release adds a brand-new toggle that synthesises keystrokes into Microsoft Teams or Discord while you dictate. The toggle is off by default. The feature can fail silently if the call app updates its hotkey behaviour or if your Utterbar crashes mid-dictation. Before turning it on for a real meeting, run a dummy call (a one-on-one with someone who knows you are testing) and confirm the banners and the call app's mic icon both behave the way the section below describes.

## What this changes

### Mute your microphone in Teams and Discord while you dictate (experimental)

Two new toggles in Settings, Advanced, Experimental, both off by default. When you turn one on, Utterbar will try to mute your microphone inside that app whenever you start a dictation, then unmute it when you stop. The point is to stop your dictation bleeding into a live call when you are talking to another window.

Each toggle has a small input where you tell Utterbar what your current mute hotkey is for that app, because Teams and Discord both let you rebind the mute key. The defaults match each app's out-of-the-box shortcut, but you must confirm or update them to match what your install actually has set:

- **Teams** ships with `Ctrl+Shift+M` for "Toggle mute". Confirm yours under Teams Settings, Keyboard shortcuts, Toggle mute.
- **Discord** does NOT ship with a default global mute hotkey. Open Discord, go to User Settings, Keybinds, click Add a Keybind, choose "Toggle Mute", and pick a key. Then click "Re-record" in Utterbar and press the same key here.

Utterbar cannot read either app's actual mute state, so after every dictation a banner appears above the floating bar asking you to confirm. The banner is not a glitch; it is the honest version of "we tried to unmute you, please look at the mic icon to make sure". The banner colours and behaviour are:

- **Active recording.** A muted-tone sub-line above the bar reads "Tried to mute Teams while recording. Confirm in Teams." (or both apps, if you have both toggles on). Sticks for the whole recording.
- **Successful restore.** A subdued "Tried to unmute you in Teams. Please confirm in Teams." line. Auto-dismisses after 5 seconds.
- **Restore may have failed.** A more visible banner with the warning copy and a click-to-dismiss "Confirmed" button. Stays until you click. A Confirmed click is just an acknowledgement; you still have to look at the call app and verify the mic icon yourself.

If Utterbar crashes between the start of a dictation and the stop, on the next launch it tries to send the unmute keystroke to any running call app it had muted, and shows the warning banner regardless of the keystroke result so you check the call apps. Stale state files (older than 24 hours) are deleted and ignored on launch; they are not the failure mode the recovery is for.

### Settings search v2 (already in the v0.18.1 beta)

This release also includes the Settings search v2 from v0.18.1: typing the name of a control (like `smart rewrite`, `teams mute`, `apps to silence`, `air gapped`, `gpu`) in the sidebar Settings search now finds the right section and shows a small hint of the control underneath. If you skipped v0.18.1, you get this in v0.18.2.

## What this does not change

- **No analytics, no telemetry.** Utterbar still sends nothing about your usage to us. The mic-mute feature is purely local.
- **No local LLM rewrite.** Smart rewrite stays the same deterministic rule-based feature it has been since v0.16.4.
- **No UI Automation reading the call app's mute button.** Utterbar deliberately does not introspect Teams or Discord to "verify" the mute state; that path is brittle, breaks silently when those apps update, and the always-on confirmation banner is the honest alternative.
- **No OS-level capture-endpoint mute.** Utterbar does not touch the global Windows microphone mute. The feature only sends the call app's own mute hotkey to that app's window.
- **No Zoom or Webex support.** This release covers Teams and Discord only. Zoom and Webex are separate iterations with their own beta soak; nothing about them ships in v0.18.2.
- **No changes to speech recognition, audio capture, microphone routing, GPU acceleration, the speech models, or the speech engine.** This is a Settings + lifecycle change only.
- **No website changes.**
- **The existing v0.17.0 setting that mutes what you HEAR from conferencing apps** (Settings, Privacy, Media) is unchanged and continues to work as before. The mic-mute toggle is a separate switch in Settings, Advanced, Experimental.
