# Utterbar 0.6.9

**Regression fix + honest scope correction on the drag visual.** v0.6.8 attempted to promote the floating-bar pill to its own compositor layer so the "scale up + accent border" drag visual would paint during Tauri's native window-move loop. That worked for the drag in principle, but the compositor-promotion hints (`will-change: transform` + `transform: translateZ(0)` + `transition: transform 160ms`) fought framer-motion's `layout`-driven inline transforms on the same element, producing a stutter in the normal open/close animation. That's unacceptable — smooth open/close is higher priority.

## What changed in 0.6.9

- **Outer pill reverted to v0.6.7 behaviour.** No compositor-promotion class on the pill. Framer-motion owns its scale + layout transitions exclusively. Open/close is smooth again.
- **Drag-lift scale (1.05× on pointer-down) is back to being framer-motion-driven.** Visible before and after the native drag, not during it. The drag-start "picks up" moment reads on pointerdown; during the native drag the pill sits at its scaled-up size until release; on release it springs back.
- **Accent ring (`.ub-drag-ring`) kept as a promoted sibling element.** It's a separate span, not the pill, so its compositor-layer hints don't conflict with framer-motion. In principle its opacity change can paint during the native drag; in practice this varies by WebView2 build + GPU driver + DWM state. Documented honestly in the CSS.

## Everything else from 0.6.8 is still in

- The v0.6.7 runtime patch (vendored `tauri-runtime-wry-2.10.1` with the `main_thread: Arc<_>` fix) continues to hold.
- `schedule_idle` phase guard — recordings no longer stop themselves mid-dictation.
- Cold-load status (`Initialising model`), collapse animation, vocabulary scale controls, position-persist diagnostics — all unchanged.

## The drag-border effect — honest status

After three real attempts (v0.6.6, v0.6.7, v0.6.8) to make "the border visibly changes colour during the native drag" reliable across all paint paths, the honest answer is that this cannot be guaranteed without degrading the normal open/close animation. The blocker is that Tauri's `startDragging()` hands the window's message pump to the OS for the duration of the drag, which starves the WebView2 renderer of paint opportunities. Anything that requires a renderer frame to take effect (React state, inline transform, any style change on a non-promoted element) will not appear until drag release. Anything that IS on its own compositor layer can in theory paint from the compositor thread during drag — but in practice this depends on whether the compositor already has a valid texture for the target layer or needs to re-rasterise, and re-rasterising needs the renderer.

What this build ships: a promoted-layer accent ring whose opacity flip may or may not paint during drag depending on the specifics of your Windows + GPU + WebView2 version. The drag-lift scale is framer-motion-driven and **will not** paint during drag. On release everything settles into the expected state cleanly. If during your use the ring genuinely appears during drag most of the time, good. If it doesn't, we've exhausted the cheap technical options; the next step would be either accepting a fallback visual (e.g. a momentary pre-drag flash that's visible at pointerdown, before startDragging takes over) or investigating a Tauri/Wry patch to wake the renderer inside the native drag loop, which is a bigger piece of work.
