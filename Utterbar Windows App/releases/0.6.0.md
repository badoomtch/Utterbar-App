# 0.6.0

Pro is live. This release introduces the paid tier — everything on Free keeps working the same, and the new Pro-only capabilities are flagged clearly inside the app so nothing feels hidden or paywalled out of nowhere.

- **Pro tier launched.** Seven capabilities now sit behind a single unlock: file transcription, the high-precision fp16 Parakeet model, authoring custom modes, re-transcribing stored dictations, local audio retention, unlimited vocabulary & replacements, and full history. Free keeps the 50 vocabulary words, 25 replacements, 100 recent history entries, and all three built-in modes.

- **Custom modes are now a Pro feature.** The three built-ins (Voice to Text, Message, Meeting) are still free and unchanged. Pro unlocks authoring your own — per-app auto-activation, your own templates, custom post-processing rules, the lot.

- **File transcription is Pro-only.** Drop any audio or video file (mp3, wav, m4a, flac, mp4, mov, mkv…) and get a local transcript next to the source file. Same Parakeet model as dictation, same privacy guarantees — nothing leaves your machine.

- **Parakeet fp16 (high-precision) is Pro-only.** Accuracy ceiling of the Parakeet family, needs a real GPU to run well. The standard compact and v3 models stay free and recommended for most people.

- **Audio retention is Pro-only.** Keep a local WAV of each dictation on disk so you can re-listen or re-transcribe later. Stored under your Recordings folder, auto-pruned on the retention window you pick, never uploaded.

- **Re-transcribe from history is Pro-only.** Run any stored raw transcript back through a different mode (e.g. original as Message → re-run as Meeting) without re-recording.

- **Vocabulary caps lifted on Pro.** Free keeps 50 custom words and 25 text replacements — enough for most people's jargon list. Over-cap entries authored on Pro are grandfathered: they stay usable and visible on deactivation, only adding new ones is blocked.

- **History shows your most recent 100 on Free.** Older dictations stay on disk — they reappear the instant you activate Pro. No export-then-re-import round-trip.

- **Email/Note/Super modes retired.** These three relied on local LLM post-processing that never shipped properly. Rather than leave silent-fallback stubs in place, they're removed. Proper LLM-powered modes return in 0.7 with the real inference backend.

- **Model comparison bars.** The Settings → Models list now shows paired accuracy / speed bars next to each variant, so picking between int8 / v3 / fp16 doesn't require parsing the descriptions against each other.

- **Ready / Idle badge for the ASR model.** Settings → Models now shows whether the model is currently resident in memory. Explains why the first dictation after launch feels slower than subsequent ones.

- **Release-email signup.** Settings → General has a new "App updates" card — drop your email to get notified when new releases land. Opt-in only, plain text email.

Fixed:
- Settings → General "Updates" card renamed to "App updates" to make it clearer it's about releases rather than OS updates.
- Mode Editor no longer offers the broken "llm" post-processing option on custom modes. Legacy entries render read-only and get cleaned up on next save.
- Model catalogue rows now round-trip their Pro flag through the installed-state merge — fp16 keeps its locked state after install/remove cycles.
