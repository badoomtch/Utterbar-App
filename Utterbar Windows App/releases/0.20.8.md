# Utterbar 0.20.8 (beta)

A beta release on top of v0.20.7 that makes model installs honest about what they are doing after the download, fixes hold-to-talk on the first press after launch, and removes the experimental punctuation toggle. Stable users remain on v0.20.0.

## Read this first

This is a beta release. Stable users stay on v0.20.0 unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates.

## What this release fixes

**Model installs now tell you what they are doing after the download.** When you installed a large model such as Whisper Turbo, the download would reach 100 percent and then appear to freeze on the byte count for up to a minute while the file was checked and unzipped, with no sign that anything was happening. It now shows an active bar and an honest label ("Checking the download" then "Unzipping, this can take a minute") so you know it is still working. The install was always completing; it just did not say so.

**Hold-to-talk and push-to-talk now work on the very first press after launch.** In v0.20.7, if you held the key immediately after opening the app, recording could start but not stop when you let go, because the recording was still getting going when you released. Now releasing the key always stops the recording, even on that first cold press.

## What this release removes

**The experimental "Restore punctuation" toggle has been removed.** It was added in the previous beta but it duplicated punctuation that the Whisper models already add on their own, and it could crash the app. It is gone. Your dictation is unaffected: Whisper continues to punctuate as it always has.

## What this release does not change

- **No change to transcription accuracy or your existing models.** Parakeet and every Whisper model, including Turbo, behave exactly as on v0.20.7.
- **No change to the App language picker, no translation changes, and no new interface languages.**
- **No change to the auto-update flow, the experimental Teams and Discord microphone mute, the licence system, or Stripe.**
- **No new outbound network calls from the app** beyond downloading a model when you choose to install one.
- **Stable users remain on v0.20.0** unless they opt into the beta channel from Settings, Updates.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Email hello@ddraig.dev with the log file and a short description.

## Beta channel

Beta channel only. Stable channel still serves v0.20.0. No release announcement email, no "latest" tag.
