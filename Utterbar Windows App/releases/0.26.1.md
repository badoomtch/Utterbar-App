# Utterbar 0.26.1 (beta)

A focused beta fix on top of v0.26.0. Stable users remain on v0.26.0.

## What this release fixes

- Utterbar no longer pastes common silence captions such as "crickets",
  "silence", "no speech", or "background noise" when you did not say
  anything.
- Pause markers such as three dots are removed instead of being pasted into
  your sentence.
- Vocabulary replacements now respect whole words. A correction intended for
  "blu" no longer changes the beginning of "blurry" into "bluerry".
- Existing corrections such as "bluee" to "blue" continue to work.
- An optional Whisper Turbo model is available for users who want a stronger
  multilingual model for everyday dictation.
- Number conversion remains safe when your sentence contains accented,
  non-Latin, or emoji text.

## What this does not change

- The default speech model, audio capture, and GPU behaviour are unchanged.
  Whisper Turbo is an optional model choice and is not selected automatically.
- Utterbar remains local-first and does not add telemetry or cloud
  transcription.
- The experimental Teams and Discord microphone-mute feature remains included
  in the release build.

This is a beta release. If a spoken word is still missing or misrecognised,
please report the exact phrase you said, the text that appeared, your selected
model, and whether the issue repeats.

## Beta channel
