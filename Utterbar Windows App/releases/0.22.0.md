# Utterbar 0.22.0

This update makes everyday dictation more reliable. It improves how Utterbar puts text into other apps, protects what you already have on the clipboard, and gives you separate controls for clipboard restoration and the end of your sentence.

## More reliable dictation delivery

Utterbar now handles failed text insertion more carefully. If Windows cannot insert a transcript into the app you were using, Utterbar does not claim that it worked. Your transcript remains available in History so you can copy it manually.

When automatic Enter is enabled, Utterbar only sends it after the text has actually been inserted. This avoids a failed paste also sending an unexpected message.

## Your copied images are restored too

If **Restore previous clipboard** is enabled, Utterbar now restores copied images as well as copied text after a dictation. Copy a screenshot or image, dictate into a text field, then paste again afterwards: the image should still be there.

Utterbar also avoids overwriting the clipboard when you or another app copy something new while it is waiting to restore the previous item.

## Two separate delay controls

There are now two controls for the two different jobs that happen when a dictation ends.

- **Clipboard restore delay** in **Settings, Text Output, Delivery** controls when Utterbar puts back what was on your clipboard before dictation. It does not delay transcription or the initial paste.
- **End-of-speech delay** in **Settings, Audio** keeps capture open briefly after you stop recording, helping include the end of your last sentence.

Both controls offer values from 250 to 1,500 ms. Start with the defaults. If final words are occasionally cut off, increase the end-of-speech delay. If the app feels slow after pasting, reduce the clipboard restore delay. A longer clipboard delay can help slower apps receive the pasted transcript before your previous copied text or image returns.

## What this release does not change

- No speech model, transcription, audio-routing, or graphics acceleration changes.
- No change to Meeting Mode beyond retaining final captured audio more reliably.
- No change to the App language picker or interface translations.
- No change to the experimental Teams and Discord microphone mute feature.
- No licence, purchase, price, update-service, website, analytics, telemetry, or local LLM changes.

## If you hit a problem

The app log is at `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\`. Email hello@ddraig.dev with the log file, your version from Settings, About, the target app, and what you expected to happen.

For a clipboard issue, say whether you copied text or an image and which clipboard restore delay you selected. For a missing final word, include the full sentence you spoke and what appeared.
