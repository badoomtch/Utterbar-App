# Utterbar 0.21.4 (beta)

A small beta on top of v0.21.3. Stable channel still serves v0.21.0.

## What this release adds

You can now adjust both delays involved when you finish a dictation.

- **End-of-speech delay** is in **Settings, Audio**. It keeps capture open for a short period after you stop recording, helping Utterbar include the end of your last sentence. Choose between 250 and 1,500 ms. The default is 500 ms.
- **Clipboard restore delay** remains in **Settings, Text Output, Delivery**. It controls when Utterbar puts back the content that was on your clipboard before dictation. This is separate from audio capture and does not delay transcription or pasting.

For end-of-speech capture, Utterbar can continue briefly when it still detects speech, up to 1,500 ms. This protects a final word without making every dictation wait at the maximum setting.

## Choosing a setting

Start with the defaults unless you are seeing a specific problem.

- If final words are occasionally cut off, increase **End-of-speech delay**.
- If the app feels slow after pasting, reduce **Clipboard restore delay**. A longer clipboard delay is more reliable for restoring copied images and other non-text content on slower apps.

## What this release does not change

- No speech model, transcription, audio-routing, or GPU behaviour changes.
- No change to when your transcript is pasted. The clipboard delay only affects restoring the previous clipboard contents afterwards.
- No change to the experimental Teams and Discord microphone mute feature.
- No new language or translation changes.
- No licence, purchase, update-service, website, analytics, telemetry, or local LLM changes.

## Beta channel
