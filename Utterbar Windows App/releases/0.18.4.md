# Utterbar 0.18.4 (beta)

A small beta hotfix on top of v0.18.3. Stable channel still serves v0.18.0.

## Read this first

**Experimental. Do not test this in a real call until you have verified it with a safe dummy call.**

## What this changes

Three narrow fixes to the experimental Teams + Discord microphone-mute toggles introduced in v0.18.2 and instrumented in v0.18.3:

- **Mic-mute hotkey delivery no longer steals focus.** When the toggle is on and a recording starts, Utterbar sends the configured mute hotkey to the system input queue rather than briefly grabbing foreground for the call app's window. Teams (Ctrl+Shift+M by default) and Discord (whatever you have set under User Settings, Keybinds, Toggle Mute) both receive the keystroke as a Windows-level global hotkey, so no window targeting is needed. As a side effect, this also means tray-minimised Discord now mutes correctly; v0.18.2 silently skipped the keystroke whenever Discord was minimised to the tray because the visible-window scan returned nothing.
- **Auto-paste after dictation works again with mic-mute on.** The previous focus-dance path raced against Utterbar's own foreground capture (the one used to know which window to paste your transcribed text into), leaving the paste pointing at an indeterminate target. The new global-hotkey send path has no foreground side effects, so the original target window keeps focus and your dictated text lands where you were typing.
- **Floating-bar layout fixed.** v0.18.3 made the bar look tiny and thin whenever the mic-mute banner was showing, because the banner was a flex sibling of the pill and squeezed the pill's width. The banner is now positioned absolutely below the pill, so the pill renders at its full natural width regardless of banner state.

If you have one of the toggles on with a hotkey set and a call app open, just press your Utterbar recording hotkey, speak briefly, and let go. Watch for the banner appearing below the bar and your transcribed text appearing in the focused app.

## What this does not change

- **No analytics, no telemetry.** Utterbar still sends nothing about your usage to us.
- **No local LLM rewrite.**
- **No UI Automation reading the call app's mute button.**
- **No OS-level capture-endpoint mute.**
- **No Zoom or Webex support.** Teams and Discord only.
- **No changes to speech recognition, audio capture, microphone routing, GPU acceleration, the speech models, or the speech engine.**
- **No website changes.**
