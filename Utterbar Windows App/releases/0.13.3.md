# Utterbar 0.13.3 (beta)

Small UX iteration on Meeting Mode. Beta channel.

## What's new

**The bar tells you what's happening during a meeting transcribe.** A 5-minute meeting takes ~17 seconds to chunk-transcribe on Parakeet. Until now, the floating bar showed the generic "Processing" pill the whole time — same as a 0.3-second dictation. The user couldn't tell whether the meeting was actually transcribing or stuck.

Now Meeting Mode emits a distinct phase, `transcribing_background`, between capture-drain and the final Success pill. The floating bar shows:

> Transcribing meeting…

…with the same accent treatment as Processing but the copy makes the state visible. After the chunks finish, the existing v0.13.2 success pill ("Saved · 5:01 · 11 chunks · 799 chars") fires as before.

## Deferred to v0.14

The fuller "queue meetings in the background while you record another one" experience needs more architectural work — the inference orchestrator's transcribe-in-flight guard exists for crash safety (it was the v0.6.2 heap-corruption fix), and removing it requires either separate ASR sessions per meeting (doubles GPU memory) or a proper job-queue with per-job audio buffers serialised through the existing write-lock. The right shape is to land that with the Meetings page redesign in v0.14.0 rather than half-shipping it here.

So for v0.13.3: starting a second meeting while the first transcribes still gets a "busy, try again" toast, same as v0.13.2. The improvement is purely cosmetic — but it's the cosmetic the testing feedback flagged ("I have no idea if it actually recorded the full 5 minutes").

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log. `crash.log` has any panic traces. Send the newest files to `hello@ddraig.dev`.
