# Utterbar 0.14.1 (beta)

Three pieces of feedback from the v0.14.0 soak. Beta channel.

## Speaker tag heuristic, rewritten

The old "loudest wins" comparison tagged every chunk as **You**. The reason was simple — your microphone picks up the audio bleeding from your speakers, so the mic's signal is "your voice + bleed-through" while loopback alone is just "their voice." Mic always wins the RMS race even when the other speaker is the dominant signal.

The new heuristic asks a different question: **was the system audio producing real signal in this chunk?** A loopback noise floor is calibrated once over the whole recording (smallest 30-second window's RMS), and each chunk is tagged **Other** if its loopback RMS is more than 4× that floor, **You** otherwise. The threshold is relative to the recording's own silence baseline so it adapts to whatever system volume you had set.

Imperfect when both sides talk simultaneously — we'd tag **Other** because the loopback signal is real — but that's a truer label than "always You."

## Whisper Large v3 — best for meetings

New entry in Settings → Models: **Whisper Large v3 (best for meetings)**. The most accurate offline ASR model in the catalogue. ~1 GB download, Pro-tier. Substantially better than Parakeet on multi-speaker audio, accents, noisy rooms — at the cost of speed (~3-4× real-time on CPU; a 30-minute meeting takes ~8-10 minutes to transcribe).

Use it specifically for meetings; for short-form dictation Parakeet is still the right pick because it's ~10× faster.

## Updates page

The Update channel + auto-check + check-now controls are now their own sidebar entry — **Settings → Updates** — instead of a card buried in General Settings. The page also shows the release notes for the version you're currently running, and a clear warning if you're on the beta channel that builds may break your install.

## If something does go wrong

`%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` has the app log. `crash.log` has any panic traces. Send the newest files to `hello@ddraig.dev`.
