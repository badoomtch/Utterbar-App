# Utterbar 0.15.10 (beta)

A small reliability fix on top of v0.15.9: Meeting Mode now filters obviously bad per-chunk output before stitching the transcript, so a single bad 29-second chunk no longer leaves a wall of junk in the middle of an otherwise good meeting.

## What this fixes

Meeting Mode transcribes a recording in 29-second chunks and stitches the results together with `[MM:SS]` timestamps. Up to v0.15.9, every chunk's text went straight to the stitched output, even if the chunk produced obvious garbage (a single period, a wall of repeated punctuation, or whitespace). One bad chunk in a 30-minute meeting meant one ugly line of timestamps in the middle of the transcript.

v0.15.10 catches the same low-false-positive cases the regular validator catches, but on a per-chunk basis. A bad chunk is skipped at stitch; the rest of the meeting goes through normally.

## What changes

- Each Meeting Mode chunk runs through a chunk-specific validator after ASR and after the boundary-overlap dedup.
- The validator drops the chunk (it does not appear in the stitched transcript) when the chunk text is empty, whitespace-only, a single non-alphanumeric character, or a long run of repeated punctuation.
- Repeated-token chunks (a Whisper decoder loop like "the the the the the the") are kept for now and only logged. Real meeting speech can carry sustained repetition (emphatic agreement, hedge speech, a stuck speaker), and the cost of dropping a real 29-second chunk is too high until soak telemetry says it is safe.
- Foreign-script and multilingual chunks are kept. Meetings that mix English with names or technical terms in another script are common and legitimate.
- One info log line per dropped or observed chunk in `%APPDATA%\Utterbar\logs\` carries the reason, chunk index, character count, chunk duration, and model so support can see which rule fired without re-running the audio.

## What this does not change

- Success pill, Meetings page, history insert, and the meeting summary all behave the same as before.
- Meeting Mode chunking, the 29-second window, the 2-second overlap, the meeting cap, and the model allow-list are all unchanged.
- Engine-internal filters (the existing Whisper hallucination list, the Parakeet filler-word gate) stay where they are and run first, as before.
- Regular dictation validation from v0.15.8 is unchanged.
- File transcription validation from v0.15.9 is unchanged.
- Pricing, licence system, updater, history persistence, the floating bar visuals.
- Local-only. No audio or transcript leaves your machine.

## Beta only

Beta-channel release. No `--announce`, no `latest` tag. Stable channel still serves v0.15.6.
